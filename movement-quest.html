The Movement Quest

You already have the answers. Now we seek to discover them together.

Choose Your Path
Welcome

This intensive is designed to help you understand how to find your own movement identity through a structured practice — and how to break down an act in a structured way.

Every performer, in all movement arts, has a different approach to act creation, and I hope that mine will be a helpful addition to your performance toolbelt.

The Guild

An online community of movers.

The Guild is an online community of humans who have gone through The Movement Quest and share their discoveries together. Members also get access to movement challenges and choreography puzzles developed specifically for the program.

The Map

Your workbook, your path.

Every guild member receives a “map” for discovering their own movement and choreography — but it is up to the member to decide their own path. Each book contains four workshops with exercises, helpful tips, diagrams, blank worksheets, and a basic glossary.
